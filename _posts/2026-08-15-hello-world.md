Every blog begins with an empty page. This one begins with a simple intention: **write down what I learn before it disappears.**

There will be practical notes here, longer ideas, and small discoveries that deserve a more durable home than a browser tab. Some posts will be polished; others will be field notes. The useful thing is to begin.

## What I want this space to be

I want a place that feels calm, loads quickly, and belongs to me. A place where the archive matters more than the feed and where a post from years ago can still be useful today.

That means a few simple principles:

1. **Clarity over cleverness.** The idea should be the interesting part.
2. **Useful over frequent.** There is no publishing quota.
3. **Curiosity over certainty.** A good question is worth recording.

> A personal site is less like a broadcast channel and more like a garden: patient, imperfect, and always becoming.

## Publishing the next post

Posts live in the `_posts` folder of this repository. To publish one, create a Markdown file named with this pattern:

```text
YYYY-MM-DD-your-post-title.md
```

Begin it with a small block of front matter:

```yaml
---
title: "Your post title"
description: "A one-sentence summary."
date: 2026-08-16 09:00:00 -0400
categories: [Notes]
reading_time: "4 min read"
---
```

Write the post below that block, commit it to `main`, and GitHub Pages handles the rest.

For now, this is enough: a first post, a working archive, and a reason to return.
